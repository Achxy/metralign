## Design Context

### Users

People opening the project’s GitHub Pages link specifically to watch its video explanation. Their only job is to identify the project, understand the video’s subject from one short line, and watch it without distraction.

### Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, and technical. The page should feel composed and credible rather than promotional, playful, or feature-heavy.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained editorial presentation on a pitch-black canvas, with warm-white typography, quiet neutral supporting text, and a generous viewing area. The interface is dark-only, responsive, and deliberately sparse. It must not use glows, gradients, decorative cards, or look like a startup landing page, dashboard, or neon sci-fi interface.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the video unmistakably primary.
2. Include only the requested title, subtitle, player, and creator credit.
3. Use spacing, proportion, and typography—not extra content or decoration—to create character.
4. Preserve excellent legibility, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, and comfortable layouts from small phones to wide desktops.
5. Keep the static site fast, dependency-free, and reliable on GitHub Pages.
